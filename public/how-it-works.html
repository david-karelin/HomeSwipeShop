How Seligo Works

Seligo makes finding room decor simple. No endless scrolling, no decision fatigue — just curated picks that match your style.

1

Swipe Through Picks

See a product you love? Swipe right or tap the heart. Not your vibe? Swipe left. Seligo learns your preferences with every swipe.

2

Build Your Shortlist

Save items to your wishlist or add them to your bag. Your shortlist stays with you so you can compare and shop when ready.

3

Scan Your Room (Optional)

Upload a photo of your space and let Seligo's AI suggest decor that complements your existing furniture, colors, and layout.

4

Shop Your Favorites

When you're ready, tap to buy. Seligo links you directly to trusted retailers like Amazon so you can complete your purchase.
