Frequently Asked Questions

What is Seligo?

Seligo is a room decor discovery app. Swipe through curated products, save your favorites, and shop when you're ready. We help you find decor that matches your style — without endless scrolling.

How does the swiping work?

Swipe right (or tap the heart) to save items you love. Swipe left to skip items that aren't your vibe. Seligo learns your preferences with every swipe and shows you more of what you like.

What is AI Room Scanning?

Upload a photo of your room and our AI analyzes your space — the colors, furniture, and layout. We then suggest decor that complements what you already have.

Is Seligo free to use?

Yes, Seligo is completely free to use. You can swipe, save, and build your shortlist without any cost.

How does Seligo make money?

Some links on Seligo are affiliate links. When you click through and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This helps us keep the app free.

Where do the products come from?

We curate products from trusted retailers like Amazon. Every item is hand-picked for style and quality. We focus on bedroom decor, living room finds, desk accessories, and small-space solutions.

Do you sell products directly?

No, we don't sell products directly. When you're ready to buy, we link you to the retailer's website where you can complete your purchase.

How do I save items?

Swipe right or tap the heart to add items to your wishlist. Tap the bag icon to add items to your shopping bag. Your saved items stay with you so you can compare and shop when ready.

Is my data private?

Your preferences are stored locally on your device. We don't require an account, and we don't share your personal data with third parties.

Can I reset my preferences?

Yes, you can reset your style profile and start fresh from the settings in your profile page.
