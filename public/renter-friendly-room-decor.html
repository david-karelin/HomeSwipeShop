Renter-Friendly Room Decor

No drilling, no painting, no problem

Renting doesn't mean living with bare walls and boring spaces. With the right products and techniques, you can personalize your rental without risking your security deposit.

Command Strips Are Your Best Friend

Heavy-duty command strips can hold mirrors, shelves, and art without drilling. Follow weight limits carefully, and remove slowly when you move out. No holes, no damage.

Peel-and-Stick Wallpaper

Removable wallpaper transforms accent walls, backsplashes, and even furniture. Choose subtle patterns or bold prints — it all comes down cleanly when you leave.

Lean, Don't Hang

Oversized mirrors and art look great leaning against walls. No hanging required. Stack frames on a console table or dresser for an effortlessly styled look.

Rugs Cover Everything

Hate your floors? Cover them with layered rugs. A large area rug anchors the room; smaller accent rugs add color and pattern. Instant upgrade, zero damage.

Tension Rods & Curtains

Tension rods let you hang curtains without drilling into window frames. Use them to divide spaces, hide closets, or add drama to windows.

Freestanding Furniture

Bookcases, room dividers, and standing shelves add storage and style without wall mounting. Look for pieces that fit your space and move with you to your next place.

Lighting Upgrades

Floor lamps, table lamps, and plug-in pendant lights transform the mood without touching ceiling fixtures. Swap in warm bulbs for that cozy glow.

Plants, Plants, Plants

Nothing personalizes a space like greenery. Plants add life, color, and personality with zero commitment to the apartment itself.
