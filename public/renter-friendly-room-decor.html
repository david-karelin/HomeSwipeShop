Renter-Friendly Room Decor

No drilling, no painting, no problem

Renting doesn't mean living with bare walls and boring spaces. With the right products and techniques, you can personalize your rental without risking your security deposit. Everything here is removable, reusable, or damage-free.

Command Strips Are Your Best Friend

Heavy-duty command strips can hold mirrors, shelves, and art without drilling. Follow weight limits carefully, and remove slowly at move-out. No holes, no damage, no deductions from your deposit. They work on painted drywall, plaster, and most smooth surfaces.

Peel-and-Stick Wallpaper

Removable wallpaper transforms accent walls, backsplashes, and even furniture. Choose subtle patterns or bold prints — it all comes down cleanly when you leave. Many renters use it on a single wall behind the bed to create a headboard effect.

Lean, Don't Hang

Oversized mirrors and art look great leaning against walls. No hanging required. Stack frames on a console table or dresser for an effortlessly styled look. Leaning decor is one of the easiest ways to make a rental feel intentionally designed.

Rugs Cover Everything

Hate your floors? Cover them with layered rugs. A large area rug anchors the room; smaller accent rugs add color and pattern. Instant upgrade, zero damage. Area rugs also help with noise in apartments with thin floors.

Tension Rods & Curtains

Tension rods let you hang curtains without drilling into window frames. Use them to divide spaces, hide closets, or add drama to windows. Floor-length curtains make ceilings look taller and rooms feel more finished.

Freestanding Furniture

Bookcases, room dividers, and standing shelves add storage and style without wall mounting. Look for pieces that fit your space and move with you to your next place. A tall bookcase filled with objects and plants can become a room's focal point.

Lighting Upgrades

Floor lamps, table lamps, and plug-in pendant lights transform the mood without touching ceiling fixtures. Swap in warm bulbs for that cozy glow. Some plug-in pendants use adhesive hooks so they hang like proper ceiling lights with no wiring required.

Plants, Plants, Plants

Nothing personalizes a space like greenery. Plants add life, color, and personality with zero commitment to the apartment itself. A fiddle leaf fig, a trailing pothos on a shelf, or a cluster of succulents on a windowsill all work beautifully.
